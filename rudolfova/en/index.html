GRAFIT / MOST · RUDOLFOVA

Rudolfova (Karla Liebknechta): the whole street in 3D from archival sources

Rudolfova (Buergermeister-Rudolf-Strasse, Karla Liebknechta from 1964) ran north from Teplicka street along the railway yard, outside the historic core of old Most. Its west side held a row of one-storey houses of 1888-1903 (the Griesbach row, the Riess houses, the Stanek house 1216), two large blocks of railway workers flats (type B1, 1924) and family houses by the builder Bartak at the north end; on the east side stood the old railway station building, a goods shed and two Theresia Tiefbau-Gewerkschaft villas of 1929. 22 houses were modelled from GIS footprints, building plans, photographs and record cards. 14 of them are documented only by a building project or only by the GIS footprint and carry a hypothesis label everywhere on these pages. The geometry is untextured; Codex adds textured houses one by one.

What stood where

Positions come from the GIS layer, a vectorisation of the 1938 town plan, which lists Rudolfova as street 63 with 23 buildings. The database area Liebknechtova holds 29 structures (live listing of 23 Oct 2026); 21 are houses of this street, no. 1167 has an empty structure, and the rest belong to Paridelska or Nadrazni or have no footprint. The GIS label 1784 on the building between 1835 and 1813 conflicts with the archive, which places no. 1824 there; the model follows the archive and states the conflict.

Sources and geometry

All 303 attachments of the 29 structures were read (read-only) and opened: photographs, record cards, a postcard, building plans from 1888 to 1932 and colourised derivatives. The houses 830, 812, 851, 865 (Griesbach 1888-1898), 1131 and 1166 (Riess 1901) have no photograph and are design hypotheses after their building projects; 1167 has no source at all. The corner inn 692 follows a pre-1918 postcard that differs from its 1891 project (mansard roof, chamfered corner with balcony). A parametric Blender 4.5 builder turned per-house JSON plans into models with real window niches, closed attics and roof shapes checked against sections and roof plans; every house passed an automatic gate (attic ray test, roof coverage, UV, mesh, facade counts) and a visual check. Heights, depths and the street profile are estimates; no historical elevation data exist.
